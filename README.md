# Flipcast

AI agents that analyze Depop sales, research resale trends, and forecast what to source and how to price it. Built with Claude Code, deployed on Netlify.

**Live site:** https://depop-sales-insights.netlify.app

Upload a Depop sales CSV (or try the demo data) to see your sales dashboard, then generate an AI report from three agents:

- **Trend Analyst:** what sells, when, and for how much
- **Market Researcher:** searches the resale market for what to source next and how to price it
- **Forecaster:** your next 30 days of sales, with a range

Stack: React + Vite + Tailwind, Netlify Functions and Blobs, Claude Opus 5.5 through Netlify AI Gateway.
